Our Services

GENERAL OVERHAUL ENGINE & GEAR BOX ▼

Engine Overhaul Procedures

  • Complete engine disassembly, inspection, and component cleaning.
  • Cylinder head, liner, piston, and connecting rod servicing.
  • Crankshaft and bearing inspection/replacement.
  • Fuel injection system overhaul, Turbocharger service and calibration.
  • Precision engine alignment (laser or optical), Vibration analysis, and NDT.
  • Commissioning, performance testing, tuning post-overhaul, and Overhaul Report.

Gear Box Repair & Maintenance

  • Diagnostic inspection, fault finding, and preventative maintenance programs.
  • Gear and bearing replacement, shaft sealing, and alignment.
  • Clutch assembly repair, lubrication system service, casing and housing repairs.
Our Advantages: Certified mechanics experienced in marine engine brands (Yanmar, MAN Diesel, Daihatsu, MAK, Cummins, Caterpillar, Mitsubishi). Fast & efficient to minimize vessel downtime. Work complies with safety standards and includes a Quality Assurance Guarantee.

HYDRAULIC, PNEUMATIC & VALVE SYSTEM ▼

Hydraulic System Maintenance

  • Repair for Cargo/Hoist/Provision Crane, Winchlass, & Mooring winch.
  • Power Pack Steering Gear, Bow Thruster, CPP, and Framo Pump repair.
  • Pump/motor repair, Valve servicing, Leak detection, and pressure testing.

Pneumatic & Valve Systems

  • Installing actuators, cylinders, valves, compressors, and air treatment systems.
  • Assembling air logic systems, piping, headers, and configuring sensors.
  • PV Valve & Safety Valve calibration (Class Certificate), Tank Gauging System.
  • Troubleshooting air leaks, replacing faulty components, and HVAC maintenance.
Our Advantages: High precision calibration tools for safety valves. Rapid response troubleshooting for pressure leaks and system failures. We provide class-certified testing to restore high-pressure system integrity safely and efficiently.

ELECTRICAL SYSTEM, PLC & AUTOMATION ▼

Generator & Alternator Services

  • Diagnostic testing, troubleshooting, and Load testing.
  • Stator and rotor rewinding, Insulation resistance testing.
  • Bearing replacement, Brush and commutator maintenance, Voltage regulator repair.

Electrical System, PLC & Calibration

  • Develop PLC, DCS, and HMI systems (System Design & Programming).
  • Installation, testing, and repair of electrical panels, wiring, sensors, and actuators.
  • Root cause analysis and troubleshooting for machinery control systems.
  • Calibration for: Oil Water Separator, ODME, Safety Device Engine, Maneuvering System, Fire Alarm, and Fixed Gas Detectors.
Our Advantages: Expert marine electrical engineers equipped with accurate diagnostic tools. 100% compliance with industry standards (NSBI) and marine safety codes to prevent critical power failures and optimize Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE).

PROPELLER, SHAFT & PUMP SERVICES ▼

Propeller and Shaft Repair

  • Visual inspection, damage assessment, and blade straightening/repair.
  • Propeller balancing (static and dynamic).
  • Surface polishing, anti-fouling preparation, and performance optimization.
  • Shaft and coupling inspection, precise installation, and alignment.

Pump and Motor Services
